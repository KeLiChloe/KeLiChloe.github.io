Welcome! I am Ke Li (李可), a PhD candidate in Decision Sciences at [INSEAD](https://www.insead.edu/), fortunate to be advised by [Spyros Zoumpoulis](https://www.insead.edu/faculty/spyros-zoumpoulis).

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and decision sciences. I pursue two complementary research agendas: **AI for Better Decisions**: developing machine learning, reinforcement learning, and optimization methods to improve decision-making under uncertainty, with applications to sequential decision-making, targeting, personalization, and marketing; and **AI for Better Science**, exploring how artificial intelligence can complement human scientists in scientific discovery and theory building. See my <a href="/research/">Research<i class="fas fa-arrow-right link-icon" aria-hidden="true"></i></a> page for details on current projects, working papers, and publications.

Before PhD, I was a reinforcement learning researcher at the Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence. I also interned at Microsoft Research Asia, ByteDance, and Shanghai AI Laboratory. 

I received my bachelor’s degree with honors in Computer Science and Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen), where I spent four incredibly memorable years that I still look back on as a lasting source of strength.
