Ke Li (INSEAD)

Welcome! I am Ke Li (李可), a PhD candidate in Decision Sciences at INSEAD, fortunate to be advised by Spyros Zoumpoulis.

My research sits at the intersection of AI, management, and decision sciences. I work on two complementary streams: 1) optimization, machine learning, and decision-aware learning, with applications to targeting, personalization, and marketing decisions, and 2) human–AI collaboration in the scientific process. See my Research page for details on current projects, working papers, and publications.

Before PhD, I was a reinforcement learning researcher at the Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence. I also interned at Microsoft Research Asia, ByteDance, and Shanghai AI Laboratory.

I received my bachelor’s degree with honors in Computer Science and Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen), where I spent four incredibly memorable years that I still look back on as a lasting source of strength for the years ahead.
